C — The Quiet Page

The least chrome of the three. The Journal opens on one quiet page: every line the world said, in order, newest at the top, older lines a little quieter (never lighter than the contrast floor), whispers and falls interleaved as plain lines among them. No tabs, no chips, no grouping — the page reads like a letter someone kept. The fading line over the world stays exactly as approved.

Falling is a warm hush: the world dims deep, one small quiet card near the bottom holds the words, and any key, tap or button continues. Nothing is asked of the player.
