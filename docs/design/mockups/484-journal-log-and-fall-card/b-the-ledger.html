B — The Ledger

The Journal grows tabs: the whispers stay on their own tab, and a Messages tab keeps a ledger of everything said — one row per line, each with what kind of thing it was (said, fight, found, overheard) and where and when it happened. Newest at the bottom, and the panel follows the newest line the way the kitchen follows its rail. The fading line over the world stays exactly as approved.

Falling shows a low, quiet card above the fight row — placed so it can never sit on Strike, the exact failure of the earlier attempt.

A ledger, not prose: every line keeps its kind, its place and its phase as real text, so a reader can skim a day of play in a minute. The Journal is one panel with one Close, opened the way the Bag opens — the log cannot fail to open behind a menu that never closed. The newest row carries a soft amber edge; the oldest rows are never hidden.
