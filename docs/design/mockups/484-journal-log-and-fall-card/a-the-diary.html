A — The Diary

The Journal keeps the whispers it has, and above them it keeps a diary of what the world said: the lines that today fade one at a time over the world (the messages stack) are written into the Journal as they happen, grouped under the place where they were said, the newest place first. The fading line over the world stays exactly as approved — this is only where the words can be read again.

Falling shows a centered calm card, in the same family as the reader and act-end cards, over a dimmed world. It never touches the fight verbs.
