      How to play

      • Move with the arrow keys or W A S D, or, on a touch screen, drag the joystick (or use the D-pad you chose in Display).
      • Use what is near you with F, Space or Enter, or the big Interact button. It always says what it will do. The same keys turn the pages of a note and close someone's speech, and F is how you close a shop.
      • Hear a rumor with Q or the Listen button.
      • Open your bag with B or the Bag button.
      • Walk the unexplored dark with O or the Explore button.
      • Turn the dark off with V, or in Display. Dusk and Dawn are in Display too, under Time of day.
      • Open and close this menu with Esc.

      Model settings

      This game can use your own language model for fresh lines. It plays fully without one.
